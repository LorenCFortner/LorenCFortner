## How I think about software

- **Tests aren't optional.** TDD isn't a box to check — it's how you find out if you actually understand the problem before you've committed to a solution.
- **Architecture is a conversation.** The best designs come from writing things down and then arguing about them with smart people.
- **Metrics matter, but pick the right ones.** Velocity points are a distraction. Test coverage trends, revert rates, and time-to-close tell you more.
- **AI is a tool, not a replacement.** The teams that will win are the ones where humans define intent and quality bars, and AI accelerates the middle.
